Facility Overview

Engineered for Pharmaceutical Excellence

Our integrated manufacturing complex houses dedicated clean rooms, formulation laboratories, QC testing suites, and advanced packaging lines — designed to ensure product integrity and eliminate cross-contamination.

  • Controlled clean room environments with HEPA filtration
  • Automated tablet compression with in-line checkweighing
  • Dedicated segregated manufacturing areas per product type
  • Compliant batch management and electronic records
  • Validated temperature and humidity controlled storage
  • High-speed packaging and serialization lines
Manufacturing Areas

Dedicated Production Zones

Segregated, controlled manufacturing areas — ensuring no cross-contamination and complete product integrity.

Solid Dosage Manufacturing

Dedicated granulation, compression, and coating suites for tablets. Multiple lines engineered for scalable output with stringent quality control.

Capsule Filling Lines

High-speed hard-gelatin capsule filling machines with integrated checkweighers and metal detection — for powder and pellet fills.

Granulation & Coating Suites

Advanced fluid-bed processors, rapid mixer granulators, and auto-coater systems for precision tablet processing and coating.

Packaging & Labelling

High-speed blister packaging, strip packaging, and bottle filling lines with integrated serialization for track-and-trace requirements.

Quality Control Lab

Equipped analytical laboratory with HPLC, GC, dissolution testers, and microbiology testing facilities — validated to pharmacopoeial methods.

R&D Center

Dedicated formulation development laboratory for pre-formulation studies, analytical method development, and stability trials.

Technology & Infrastructure

HVAC Systems

Validated HVAC systems maintaining precise temperature, humidity, and pressure differentials across all clean room zones.

Water Systems

ERP & Batch Systems

Energy Efficiency

Facility engineered with heat recovery systems — designed for sustainable manufacturing with minimal environmental impact.
