Manufacturing Capabilities

Commercial manufacturing across core pharmaceutical dosage forms — engineered to rigorous quality standards.

Tablets

Comprehensive solid dosage tablet manufacturing with automated compression and advanced aqueous & organic film coating systems.

  • Uncoated, film-coated, and enteric-coated tablets
  • Chewable and sustained-release formulations
  • 100% in-line weight check and blister packaging

Capsules

High-speed automatic hard-gelatin capsule filling with precise powder, pellet, and multi-particulate combination dosing.

  • Hard gelatin capsule filling (all standard sizes)
  • Powder, pellet, and modified-release fills
  • Integrated metal detection and weight verification

Softgels

Specialized soft gelatin encapsulation line engineered for liquid, suspension, and oil-based active formulations.

  • Rotary die encapsulation technology
  • Liquid, paste, and oil filling capabilities
  • Controlled humidity drying and laser sorting
